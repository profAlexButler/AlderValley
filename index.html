A Well-Rounded Business Education

Our MBA prepares you to think critically, lead teams, and make sound decisions in a global business environment.

Whether you are completing your bachelor's degree, finishing a master's in a technical field, or looking to advance in your current career, the Alder Valley MBA can help you reach your goals. Our accredited program brings together experienced faculty, a supportive community, and students from a wide range of undergraduate backgrounds and co-op experiences.

Eleven core courses build your foundation in accounting, finance, operations, marketing, analytics, strategy, and leadership. Five electives let you go further in the areas that matter most to you, including STEM courses from across the university.

Pathways Into the MBA

+1 MBA

Stay on after your bachelor's degree and complete your MBA right away. We have +1 agreements with more than 20 partner schools in the region, and scholarships are available to +1 MBA students. Most of our recent classes are +1 students, bringing a wide variety of undergraduate degrees and co-op experience into the program.

Learn about the +1 MBA

M.S. to MBA

Complete an MBA in one year after your master's degree. Five of your M.S. courses fulfill the MBA electives, the application is shorter, merit scholarships continue at the same level, and you are assigned a dedicated MBA advisor.

Beyond the Classroom

National Black MBA Association Conference

MBA students travel to this major networking and recruiting event, with about 10,000 participants and more than 300 companies. The school provides scholarship support for conference fees, travel, and lodging.

Health Systems in Sweden and Denmark

This course compares health care models across countries, with a week of work in Sweden over spring break and a stop in Copenhagen. Students also apply design thinking on projects with regional health care organizations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I waive any courses?

Students may have up to 18 credits (6 courses) waived if they have completed appropriate undergraduate courses with grades of B or better.

When and where are courses offered?

The 11 core courses are offered in the Fall, Spring, and Summer. All core courses are offered live on campus, as well as at least once per year online.

Do you support international students?

Yes. We work closely with the International Student Services office to facilitate practical training opportunities.

What does the program cost?

Please contact Graduate Admissions for current tuition and financial aid information.
